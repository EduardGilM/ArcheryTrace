Preset photos bundled with ArcheryTrace. They are freely licensed.
None of them were generated by an AI model. The card itself is drawn in code.
Each file is a crop of the original, resized to 1080 px wide and stored as a JPEG
data URL inside a .js file, so the card canvas can export it from file:// pages.
